# OpenCTEM Corporate Contributor License Agreement

**Version {{ site.data.cla.version }}, effective {{ site.data.cla.effective_date }}**

Thank you for your interest in OpenCTEM (the "Project"), the software
maintained in the repositories of the `openctemio` organization on GitHub. To
keep clear the rights in the contributions the Project receives, a
corporation whose employees contribute to the Project as part of their work
accepts this agreement on behalf of the corporation. It protects the
Corporation as a contributor and protects the Project and its users. It does
not change the Corporation's rights to use its own Contributions for any other
purpose.

The Corporation keeps the copyright in its Contributions. This agreement
grants a license; it does not transfer ownership.

The Project is maintained by **{{ site.data.cla.grantee }}** (the "Grantee").

## How to sign

1. A person authorized to sign on behalf of the Corporation completes the
   details below and Schedule A, signs, and sends a scan or an electronically
   signed copy to **{{ site.data.cla.corporate_contact }}** with the subject
   "OpenCTEM Corporate CLA".
2. Each Designated Employee also posts the CLA comment on their first pull
   request, as described in
   [Contributing](../developers/contributing.md#contributor-license-agreement).
   That links their GitHub account to the Corporation's agreement.
3. To add or remove Designated Employees later, the CLA Manager named below
   sends an updated Schedule A to the same address.

## 1. Definitions

"You" (or "Your") shall mean the copyright owner or legal entity authorized by
the copyright owner that is making this Agreement with the Grantee. For legal
entities, the entity making a Contribution and all other entities that
control, are controlled by, or are under common control with that entity are
considered to be a single Contributor. For the purposes of this definition,
"control" means (i) the power, direct or indirect, to cause the direction or
management of such entity, whether by contract or otherwise, or (ii)
ownership of fifty percent (50%) or more of the outstanding shares, or (iii)
beneficial ownership of such entity.

"Contribution" shall mean the code, documentation or any original work of
authorship, including any modifications or additions to an existing work,
that is intentionally submitted by You to the Grantee for inclusion in, or
documentation of, any of the products owned or managed by the Grantee as part
of the Project (the "Work"). For the purposes of this definition, "submitted"
means any form of electronic, verbal, or written communication sent to the
Grantee or its representatives, including but not limited to communication on
electronic mailing lists, source code control systems (including pull
requests), and issue tracking systems that are managed by, or on behalf of,
the Grantee for the purpose of discussing and improving the Work, but
excluding communication that is conspicuously marked or otherwise designated
in writing by You as "Not a Contribution."

This Agreement also covers every Contribution that You, or any of Your
Designated Employees on Your behalf, submitted to the Project before You
accepted it.

You represent that each of Your Contributions is Your original creation (see
section 7 for submissions on behalf of others).

WARRANTIES OR CONDITIONS OF ANY KIND, either express or implied, including,
without limitation, any warranties or conditions of TITLE, NON-INFRINGEMENT,
MERCHANTABILITY, or FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 7. Work that is not your own

Should You wish to submit work that is not Your original creation, You may
submit it to the Grantee separately from any Contribution, identifying the
complete details of its source and of any license or other restriction
(including, but not limited to, related patents, trademarks, and license
agreements) of which you are personally aware, and conspicuously marking the
work as "Submitted on behalf of a third-party: [named here]".

## 8. Licensing of the Work

The Grantee may license the Work, including Your Contributions, under any
license terms, including open source licenses and commercial or other
proprietary licenses, and may change those terms at any time. The Grantee is
not obliged to use or include any Contribution.

Each Contribution that the Grantee includes in a released version of the
Project remains available, in that version, under the open source license
that the receiving repository carried when it was submitted.

## 9. Assignment

The Grantee may assign or transfer this Agreement, and all rights and
licenses granted under it, in whole or in part, to any successor legal
entity, including a company formed to hold or operate the Project, without
Your further consent. From the assignment, the successor holds the rights of
the Grantee under this Agreement.

## 10. Changes

It is Your responsibility to notify the Grantee when any change is required
to the list of Designated Employees authorized to submit Contributions on
behalf of the Corporation, or to the Corporation's Point of Contact with the
Grantee. You agree to notify the Grantee of any facts or circumstances of
which You become aware that would make these representations inaccurate in
any respect.
