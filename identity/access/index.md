# Who can see and do what

These pages explain the access situations that most often surprise owners and administrators, and
what to do about each one.

- [Roles, teams and what members can see](roles-teams-and-data.md): roles decide what a person can
  do, teams decide which assets they see.
- [Outside members](outside-members.md): people with a personal address or an address from another
  organization, and how to give them more than the member role.
- [Two-factor requirements](two-factor-requirements.md): which rules demand a second factor, what
  the person sees, and how to recover.
- [Why was I refused?](refusals.md): the messages the console shows when a change to roles or teams
  is refused, with the cause and the fix.
- [AI assistants and data access](ai-assistants-and-data.md): why a connected assistant may see
  less than you do in the console.

For the full permission model see [Roles, groups and permissions](../roles-and-permissions.md).
